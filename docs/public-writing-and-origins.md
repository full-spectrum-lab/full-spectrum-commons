# Public Writing and Origins

Full Spectrum has both an engineering track and a public-writing track. They support different purposes.

## Public reading

- [《开窗手册：全频谱、接口与静默封神》](https://read.douban.com/column/72712765/) — narrative origins and public-facing writing behind the project.
- [知乎专栏](https://www.zhihu.com/column/c_2008500556897998127) — Chinese essays on AI governance, civilization dynamics, and the Full Spectrum framework.
- [Official website](https://fullspectrumprotocol.com/index.html) — public overview, architecture, protocol guide, and selected use cases.

## Boundary

These materials provide intellectual background, narrative context, and public explanation. They are not normative protocol specifications, software contracts, peer-reviewed scientific evidence, regulatory guidance, or proof of implemented capability.

For normative definitions, use [full-spectrum-protocol](https://github.com/full-spectrum-lab/full-spectrum-protocol). For runnable behavior, use [full-spectrum-engine](https://github.com/full-spectrum-lab/full-spectrum-engine).
