# Full Spectrum: understand the whole, then recursion

[简体中文](./whole-system-and-recursion.zh-CN.md)

Full Spectrum seeks shared principles and structures through which different subjects can collaborate, form larger wholes, and evolve through real-world feedback while retaining their differences, boundaries and responsibilities. It is not a central platform absorbing every participant or four unrelated tools that gain value only when assembled.

This is an explanatory document for review, not a source of mandatory rules. Non-normative does not mean worthless, nor does it define the whole system as a document. The overall mechanisms here are design explanations; existing product implementations and releases require their own pinned evidence.

## Principles and engineering support

This guide follows the owner's clarification recorded in QPP44: **the Compassion Protocol is the highest principle; the Compassion Charter supplies constraints.** Engineering carries those principles and constraints into bounded mechanisms rather than replacing them.

The QPP38 A-version introduction describes the roots differently, as the Charter supplying direction and the Protocol supplying concrete rules. We retain that source difference; this guide does not claim QPP38 was harmonized, a normative version changed, or version B activated. It adds no Charter clauses. The Compassion Protocol is not synonymous with the software repository named Protocol.

| Product | Plain-language responsibility |
|---|---|
| Protocol | Identity, authorization, boundaries and responsibilities between subjects |
| Engine | Reproducible evaluation under supported fixed inputs and rules |
| Knowledge Governance (KG) | Which exact knowledge and version was used, its source and applicability |
| Observer | Authorized real-world tasks and facts connected to evidence, audit, replay and review records |

These are independent responsibilities, not four mandatory processes or installation steps. See [four independent engineering tracks](./four-independent-engineering-tracks.md).

The overall model combines subject scales, the knowledge axis, protocol relationships, reality/evidence intersections, and CASE/Pack/Adapter/Skill domain extensions. They constrain the same problem together rather than forming a product pipeline.

## Recursion: a subject can be both a node and a system

Isomorphism here first means a shared design grammar: subjects and boundaries, inputs and state changes, influence through relationships, responsibility and evidence, conflict and unknowns, review and repair, exit and evolution.

Different scales retain that structure and its constraints while using different knowledge, parameters, membership relations and independent histories. This does not prove mathematical isomorphism or require identical instances.

L1–L4 are scales in a particular relationship, not permanent job ranks or subject types. A formed network can become a node in an outer relationship while retaining its internal structure, member boundaries and history. Greater scope does not imply greater personal worth or unlimited authority.

One cross-scale analysis can be explained as follows:

1. Lower-level results and evidence remain stored; authorized stable references carry conflicts, unknowns and review limitations.
2. A larger subject declares its own question, boundary and membership, adding its facts, knowledge, resources and rules.
3. It evaluates its own relationship risks rather than copying conclusions or averaging scores.
4. Claims can be traced under applicable visibility rules. Invisibility does not mean absence, but even existence disclosure can be restricted.
5. Feedback creates new records and runs; corrections refer to earlier judgments without overwriting history.

This reading order is not a mandatory runtime sequence. Local cycles, horizontal collaboration, cross-scale influence and feedback coexist. A path stops when required inputs, contracts or permissions are missing. See the [synthetic refund example](./examples/refund-conflict-recursion.md).

## What remains stable and what may change

The evidence plane preserves fact identity, provenance and stable references. Cross-layer context combines permitted lower-level references with local materials. Report views serve particular questions and readers; their emphasis may change without rewriting underlying facts.

A Runestone is a structured governance record formed under a specific contract, associating results and evidence. It is not an all-purpose record or permission credential. Critical counterevidence and UNKNOWN must not disappear through summarization. A Loss Certificate describing selection, omissions and reconstruction limits remains a research candidate, not a frozen schema or a universally implemented runtime.

Core has two meanings: shared system-level grammar, and the engineering design that reuses one Engine Core with different Profiles and contexts. Existing local computation is not merely conceptual; it does not establish complete recursive integration.

Contract may mean relationship/responsibility terms, authorization and mutual-recognition conditions, or data/interface contracts. Specify which one applies. Interface compatibility is not authorization; a contract does not imply smart-contract execution.

## The triune approach: observation, inference and self-review

In everyday terms: what informs observation, how inference proceeds, and how the analyst checks their own position and blind spots while accepting feedback for calibration.

- Data/field basis separates facts, processing results, model indicators, assumptions and gaps; not every task must use Civilization Weather.
- Process methods include v1.3 positioning, collection, evaluation, transmission, shock diagnosis, synthesis and intervention proposals where applicable.
- Meta-level review begins with the analyst's position, continues through affected parties and alternatives, and ends with limits and an invitation to correction.

v5.0 has its own five-step method; the fusion drafts also reorganize stages. This guide does not merge them into a unique workflow or mandate a macro-analysis format for every API.

Observation, inference, versioned reports, real-world consequences, cases/counterexamples, method calibration and renewed observation form an evolutionary design. Rule changes and real actions require separate authority and gates, not automatic online learning. Deterministic computation and generated reports are recorded separately; replay does not promise identical generated wording.
