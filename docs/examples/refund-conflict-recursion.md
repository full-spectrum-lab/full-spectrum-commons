# How can a refund conflict reveal a larger relationship problem?

[简体中文](./refund-conflict-recursion.zh-CN.md) · [Whole-system guide](../whole-system-and-recursion.md)

This is a synthetic teaching example with no real company, order or amount. No refund is executed; it is not an execution record for CASE005. The judgments below are design expectations. Pinned inputs, outputs and tests for this example have not been executed.

## A promise before its basis is complete

Platform terms allow a refund if condition A holds; merchant rules prohibit refunds for this order category. There is no evidence establishing A, yet the support AI has promised a refund.

One reviewer asks whether support exceeded its authority; another asks why approval cannot keep up; an organizational reviewer asks whether departments persistently use different rules. These are relationship questions about the same event, not a hierarchy of truth.

Here L1 denotes the support subject, L2 its refund collaboration, L3 the company, and L4 a network of independent organizations. They are scales under this example's boundaries, not four mandatory stages. A formed whole can become an outer node without erasing member boundaries.

## Local operation: keep the unknown explicit

Observer organizes authorized conversations and order facts. KG binds rule sources, versions, applicability and conflicts. Protocol describes permission to promise and associated responsibility. Engine evaluates under a supported Profile.

The design should retain UNKNOWN for A, the rule conflict, possible overreach and a need for human review. UNKNOWN is not zero risk or automatic refund approval/refusal. Actual outputs depend on the responsible product contracts; a story is not proof of computation.

Results and supporting evidence remain separate. A Runestone may associate them, but is not all source material or a refund credential.

## Collaboration: healthy individuals can still have a faulty connection

L2 references permitted lower-level results, evidence, unknowns and review limits, adding handoff relations, approval authority, queues and propagation timing.

Promises that occur immediately while approvals cannot verify them may create relationship risk. Averaging healthy individuals' scores does not answer that question; one anomaly does not establish system-wide failure either.

Support, refund approval and knowledge publication can also exchange permitted references horizontally. Updated rules with stale caches may conflict at the connection; relationships need not wait for upward reporting.

## Organization: add local facts and evaluate its own question

L3 adds departmental rules, resources, responsibility anchors and publication history to examine consistency among promises, budget, legal terms and knowledge release. The issue may be different interpretations of effective dates, not an employee's inevitable fault.

This is local reevaluation, not copied reporting or a raised label. Different upper-level conclusions do not overwrite earlier lower-level results, automatically assign blame or issue business commands.

Cross-company review additionally requires identity, authorization, mutual recognition, visibility and exchange contracts. Internal success does not establish external recognition. A missing gate stops that path; an L4 label does not create a live network.

## Who decides, and how is the record kept?

| Question | Boundary in this example |
|---|---|
| Who decides the refund? | The subject with actual refund authority; an authorized business system executes within scope |
| Who changes rules? | An authorized rule owner; approval of effectiveness is a separate permission |
| Who reads originals? | Visibility is checked for each reference; higher scope grants no automatic access |
| What shows what happened? | Evidence, Audit, Replay and review records under explicit contracts; Observer supports its bounded evidence responsibilities |
| Who obtains authority from a report? | Nobody: reports, Runestones and evaluation results do not issue action rights |

A real company must provide an authority matrix, limits and conditions, handoff/conflict-suspension procedures, effective dates and rules for older transactions. This story cannot invent who signs a particular refund or which version governs an old order.

## Trace and correct without rewriting history

Trace an organizational claim to permitted lower-level results, checking time, versions, evidence references and visibility. Without original access, use a permitted view or stop; even existence disclosure may be restricted.

New evidence about A or a rule error creates an additional record and a new pinned snapshot, followed by a new run under local contracts. A reversed judgment must refer to the old conclusion, supporting evidence, applicability time and reviewer. Historical records remain.

Feedback can also support proposals to calibrate rules or adjust institutions, but changing rules, approving effectiveness and taking action each require their own authority. Successful reevaluation is not automatic action approval, online learning or historical overwrite.

## The triune approach in this example

The data basis is authorized business facts and rules, not a claimed Civilization Weather calculation. Process methods help position the question, inspect evidence and analyze transmission and consequences. Meta-level review requires the analyst's position and interests, attention to burdens on customers, support and reviewers, and openness to correction.

Different reports may emphasize handoffs, financial exposure or organizational burden without deleting critical UNKNOWN or counterevidence. Reports do not replace structured computation.

## What this does and does not explain

The example explains local cycles, horizontal relationships, cross-scale reevaluation, evidence tracing and feedback. It does not establish implemented automatic cross-layer Context, uniform drill-down, Loss Certificates or a complete L1–L4 runtime. It does not prove named-company adoption, general compatibility or production readiness.
