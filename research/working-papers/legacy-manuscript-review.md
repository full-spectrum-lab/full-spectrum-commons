## Publication policy

The project will not use an unpublished manuscript as a credibility badge. A manuscript becomes a public working paper only after:

1. citation verification;
2. claim-to-evidence mapping;
3. explicit limitations;
4. terminology alignment with the current repositories;
5. author approval of the edited version;
6. a stable version and change log.
