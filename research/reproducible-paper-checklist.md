# Reproducible Working Paper Checklist

- [ ] The paper makes one limited, falsifiable claim and states non-claims.
- [ ] Code revision/release, environment and seed are pinned.
- [ ] Input fixture and expected output are committed.
- [ ] Schema version and validation command are stated.
- [ ] Test or experiment runs from a clean checkout.
- [ ] CI or a machine-readable result is linked.
- [ ] Negative and boundary cases are included.
- [ ] Limitations and threats to validity are stated.
- [ ] Working-paper and peer-review status are not conflated.

An essay, roadmap or diagram may motivate a paper, but is not experimental evidence by itself.
