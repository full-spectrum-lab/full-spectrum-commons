## 1. What is Full Spectrum?

Full Spectrum is an evolving open-source effort around:

- AI agent governance semantics
- local-first governance runtime
- enterprise AI inspection and review patterns
- cross-repository public coordination assets

Its overall design includes autonomous subjects, horizontal relationships, larger contexts that recompute their own judgments, and feedback that preserves history. The four independent engineering responsibility domains support this design; they are not a mandatory linear stack or its complete definition. Architecture direction does not prove that a complete recursive runtime or cross-organization network is implemented.

## 3. What should I expect today?

Current public state:

- `full-spectrum-protocol`: public preview of protocol definitions and schemas
- `full-spectrum-engine`: `v1.4.0` stable and `v1.5.0` enterprise-pilot candidate pre-release; production-complete governance is not claimed
- `full-spectrum-observer`: `v0.4.0-beta` latest preview and `v0.3.0-maintenance.6` maintenance candidate; production ready `NO`
- `full-spectrum-knowledge-governance`: `v0.2.0-alpha` Windows x64 pre-release candidate; production ready `NO`
- `full-spectrum-enterprise-governance`: enterprise governance cases and deployment framing in public preview
- `full-spectrum-commons`: shared maps, diagrams, and public navigation

## 4. What should I not assume?

Do not assume:

- all ideas are already production-ready
- every case represents a real deployment
- diagrams are equal to finished software
- protocol language and engine implementation are already complete

The right reading stance is: public preview, structured exploration, open iteration.
