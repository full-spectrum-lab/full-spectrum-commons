场景：企业将“签收后 7 天可自动退款”调整为“高风险商品必须人工复核”

1. 现场规则变化

企业责任人 + FDE

输入：已批准的新退款政策

绑定：生效时间、负责人、影响范围

禁止：口头通知直接覆盖旧规则

2. Observer

受控现实输入

采集已授权政策变更和待处理订单事实。

保留来源，不把观察事实改写成业务判断。

输出：无损证据投影

3. Engine

契约约束内计算

按固定模型、策略和错误语义评估订单。

识别“高风险商品”，返回人工复核要求。

输出：建议，不直接退款

4. KG

知识快照与审计

保存新旧政策快照、父子版本和决策引用。

持久化输入、结果和摘要；篡改时失败关闭。

输出：审计 envelope

5. 人工批准

命名的业务责任人

复核订单事实、政策版本和 Agent 建议。

批准、拒绝或要求补证；责任不转移给 Agent。

输出：签署的决定

6. 回放与交接

后续团队 / 审计

按当时提交、知识快照和授权重建判断。

比较变更前后结果，并解释差异来自哪里。

输出：可复核交接

身份锚点Agent、模型、策略、工具、提交
知识锚点政策来源、版本、快照、生效时间
授权锚点观察、建议、执行和写回权限分离
证据锚点原始输出、退出码、摘要、限制和人工签署

!失败关闭规则

缺少知识快照、授权或审计持久化

不得把 Agent 建议升级为可执行结果；保留 `UNKNOWN / BLOCKED` 和原始失败证据。

政策再次变化或人员交接

触发重新验证；历史结果不覆盖，新团队必须能重建当时上下文。

FDE 负责把变化带入系统；Full Spectrum 负责让变化有身份、有边界、有证据、有人批准、能够回放。

Turning a Field Policy Change into a Governed Agent Change

Business-realistic synthetic scenario: refund-policy evaluation, approval, and replay

Updated 2026-09-10 10:48
Scenario: “automatic refund within seven days” changes to “high-risk products require human review”

1. Field change

Enterprise owner + FDE

Input: approved refund policy

Bind: effective time, owner, affected scope

Reject: verbal change overwriting history

2. Observer

Bounded reality input

Captures the authorized policy change and order facts.

Preserves sources without turning observations into business decisions.

Output: lossless evidence projection

3. Engine

Contract-bound compute

Evaluates the order using pinned model, policy, and error semantics.

Detects high-risk products and requires human review.

Output: advice, not a refund action

4. KG

Knowledge snapshot & audit

Stores old/new policy snapshots, lineage, and decision references.

Persists inputs, result, and digest; tampering fails closed.

Output: audit envelope

5. Human approval

Named business owner

Reviews order facts, policy version, and Agent advice.

Approves, rejects, or requests evidence; responsibility stays human.

Output: signed decision

6. Replay & handover

Successor team / audit

Rebuilds the decision using original commits, snapshot, and authority.

Compares before/after results and explains the cause of change.

Output: reviewable handover

Identity anchorAgent, model, policy, tool, commit
Knowledge anchorPolicy source, version, snapshot, effective time
Authority anchorObserve, advise, execute, and writeback are separate
Evidence anchorRaw output, exit code, digest, limitations, human signature

!Fail-closed rules

Missing snapshot, authority, or audit persistence

Do not promote Agent advice into an executable result. Preserve `UNKNOWN / BLOCKED` and raw failure evidence.

Policy changes again or ownership transfers

Trigger re-verification. Preserve history and require the successor team to rebuild the original context.

FDE brings change into the system; Full Spectrum gives that change identity, boundaries, evidence, human approval, and replay.
